# x86 CPU matrix: snarkrs vs rapidsnark, 2026-10-10

Source e8449f9 (IFMA batch-affine MSM, per-window batch). rapidsnark 81eddf1 static build, warm
wrapper. One disposable EC2 instance per type (us-east-1, Ubuntu 22.04), runner
`bench/aws/matrix.py`. Warm proving, all logical CPUs for both provers (rapidsnark's reported
ffiasm pool matched the vCPU count on every box), 10 reps, median. Our every rep self-verified; our
proof plus rapidsnark's first and last proof per circuit passed snarkjs 0.7.6 locally with public
signals equal to the reference: 270/270 checks. ADX build on every host (all have ADX/BMI2).

Not bare metal at the high end: the account's on-demand vCPU quota is 64, so c5.metal, c7i.metal-48xl,
c8a.metal-48xl and c8i.metal-96xl were refused; the largest sizes under 64 vCPU stood in.

## Findings

- We beat rapidsnark on every machine and circuit: 1.02x to 5.53x.
- IFMA is the divider. Without it (m5zn, c5a, c6a, c5) the gain is 1.02x to 2.47x and small circuits
  on 4 to 8 vCPU are nearly tied. With it (c6i, c7i, c7a, c8a, c8i) 1.47x to 5.53x.
- Same CPU, more cores: c7i 16 to 64 vCPU, our anon-aadhaar 912 to 323 ms (2.8x), rapidsnark 2345 to
  1321 ms (1.8x). Our gap widens with core count on the large circuits.
- Fastest box: c8a.16xlarge (Zen 5, 64 cores, no SMT): anon-aadhaar 199 ms vs rapidsnark 1009 ms.
- With IFMA the NTT is now as big as or bigger than the MSM on anon-aadhaar (c6i 977 vs 909 ms,
  c7i.4xlarge 476 vs 404 ms). IFMA for the NTT (TASKS X86-03) is the next lever.
- js_16x16_d32 has the lowest speedup on the large boxes (2.2x to 2.9x).
- Memory: our peak RSS is higher on every circuit, 1.2 to 1.7 GiB vs about 1 GiB on anon-aadhaar,
  and up to 6x on the small circuits, and it grows with core count.

## Caveats

- One run per instance type, not interleaved repeats; both provers ran back to back on the same host.
- Warm only. rapidsnark's warm call re-parses the witness inside the timed region; ours does not.
- High-core rows are VMs (Nitro), not bare metal; no two-socket/NUMA host was measured.
