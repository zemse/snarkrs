# x86 assembly for raw.rs, and main on perf arkworks, c7i: A/B/C/D, 2026-10-13

X86-08 and X86-05: main's NTT on `RawFr` was 7 to 13% slower than stock ark-ff `Fr` on x86
(`arkab-x86-cuda-2026-10-12.md`), because x86_64 ran `raw.rs`'s portable Rust while aarch64 had
assembly. Two fixes were on the table: (a) keep x86 on ark-ff butterflies, (b) x86 assembly for
`raw.rs`. Measured on the box, (b) wins by a wide margin, so (b) is what landed: `mulx`/`adcx`/`adox`
`mont_mul` and cmov-reduced `add_mod`/`sub_mod` in `crates/field/src/raw/x86_64.rs`, written from
scratch, built when bmi2 and adx are on (the release builds' `+adx,+bmi2`). The IFMA MSM path is
untouched. Four snarkrs builds, one toolchain, one set of flags (`bench/scripts/arkab-build.sh x86`):

- **base**: 586f42d on arkworks `v0.5.0` (7ad88c4), the main this task started from.
- **main**: 4c7f91b on `v0.5.0`: base plus the Mac round-three work (G2 batch inversion through
  norms, radix-8 NTT head, stride-packed twiddles). Separates what that branch did on x86 from
  what the assembly does.
- **fix**: e928607 (main plus the x86 assembly) on `v0.5.0`.
- **fixperf**: e928607 on `perf/bn254-field-0.5` (651414b). The "main on perf arkworks" item.

Runner `bench/scripts/arkab.py`, four variants, order reversed each round, 3 rounds, same steps
and statistics as the 2026-10-12 x86 report: prove is `snarkrs bench --mode warm`, 10 reps per
round, every rep self-verified, cell = median of all 30; verify is `verify_time`, 500 in-process
calls per round; each ceremony step is one CLI process's wall clock, cells the median of the 3
round medians. One disposable c7i.4xlarge (`bench/aws/arkab-ec2.py`), nothing else on it.

## Findings

- **The assembly is a whole-pipeline change on x86, not an NTT fix.** Against main, prove is -16
  to -22%: the NTT stage drops 26 to 32% (anon-aadhaar 403 to 275 ms, the 2^18 circuits 44 to 31
  ms) and the MSM 12 to 21% (js_16x16_d32 211 to 174 ms). The MSM gain comes from its scalar
  `RawFq` arithmetic; the IFMA fill code is unchanged, and the stage was not broken down
  further. The ceremony steps built on GLV ladders and `mul_batch` gain 24 to 27% (ptau
  contribute, ptau prepare, zkey contribute), `groth16 setup` 14%, the verifiers 8 to 16%.
- **The X86-08 regression is gone with room to spare.** The 2026-10-12 report had main's NTT 7 to
  13% slower than stock ark-ff; the microbench now has it 30% faster than ark-ff butterflies on
  stock arkworks and 6% faster than on perf arkworks.
- **First cut lost on latency, and why.** `fix1` read `a`, `b` and the modulus through pointers to
  save registers. Its dependent-chain multiply was 25 ns against ark-ff's 18: the caller copies
  `[u64; 4]` arguments with 16-byte moves, and a 16-byte load of data just written by two 8-byte
  stores cannot be store-forwarded, so every chained multiply paid a forwarding stall. The
  portable add/sub were the other half: LLVM turns their u128 borrows into an sbb/add/adc/sar
  group per limb. Keeping `a` in registers, reading the modulus and `n0` RIP-relative from a
  static, and writing add/sub in assembly fixed both.
- **main on perf arkworks (fixperf) gets both wins.** Verify -31 to -36% vs base, the same as
  perf arkworks alone gave on 2026-10-12 (-34%), while prove, prepare, setup and zkey contribute
  match fix within noise (-0.8%, +0.3%, -1.0%, -0.3%). `RawFr`/`RawFq` do not use ark-ff
  arithmetic, so perf only reaches the paths still on ark-ff: the pairing (verify), `ptau verify`
  (-7.5% vs fix) and `zkey verify init` (-2.4%). fixperf's `ptau contribute` +5.6% is the 2^13 cell
  (333 vs 300 ms, with fix's own rounds spanning 284 to 353 ms); at 2^19 the two are equal (9019
  vs 9006 ms).
- **main (the Mac round-three branch) on x86 is a small win**: prove -2.5% median (NTT stage -4 to
  -10% from the radix-8 head and packed twiddles), `ptau prepare` -5%, contribute -4.4%, the rest
  within noise. The G2 norm inversion does not show on x86 the way it does on the Mac.
- Absolute numbers are lower than the 2026-10-12 report's (base prove js_16x16_d32 275 ms against
  main's 326 there): base is 586f42d, which carries the batch-affine MSM and ceremony work landed
  since 1f85bd4.

  Runner failures: 0.

  nothing in `raw/x86_64.rs` on either target (the remaining field/ntt warnings predate this).

## Problems

- The coordinator moved main mid-task (586f42d to 4c7f91b). The microbench decision was made on

  above); the conclusion did not change.

  their inputs on the box. Those paths are covered by the A/B's snarkjs and determinism gates.
